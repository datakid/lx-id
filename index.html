14 digits, six meanings

      
Every Egyptian national ID packs birth date, sex, and place of registration into a single number. Here's how the positions break down.

    

    

      

        

          1
          

            
Century digit

            
Digit 1. 2 means born in the 1900s, 3 means born in the 2000s.

          

        

      

      

        

          2
          

            
Birth date

            
Digits 2–7, as YY MM DD. Combined with the century digit this gives the full birth date.

          

        

      

      

        

          3
          

            
Governorate code

            
Digits 8–9. Governorate/province-of-birth code assigned by the civil registry — see the Governorates tab for the full list.

          

        

      

      

        

          4
          

            
Serial number

            
Digits 10–13. A four-digit sequential registration number for that birth date and governorate; its last digit (digit 13) also encodes gender.

          

        

      

      

        

          5
          

            
Gender digit

            
Digit 13. Odd means Male, even means Female.

          

        

      

      

        

          6
          

            
Check digit

            
Digit 14. A verification digit issued with the card; this tool records it but doesn't independently validate it.

Egypt's phased retirement age increase

      

      
The standard pension age of 60 steps up gradually until it reaches 65. The age that applies to a given person depends on their birth date.

How the calendar & prayer tools work

      

      
Everything below runs as arithmetic in your browser — nothing is looked up from a server. That also means each figure is an estimate of a real-world convention, not a live authority. Details and caveats follow.

    


    

      
Hijri (Islamic) dates

      

        
remove
Uses the standard tabular Islamic calendar (a fixed 30-year leap-year cycle) — the same well-established formula used across many calendar libraries.

        
remove
This is an estimate. The real Hijri calendar is set by moon sighting, so the officially announced date — especially for Ramadan and the two Eids — can differ by a day or two.

      

    


    

      
Coptic dates, Easter & Sham El Nessim

      

        
remove
Coptic dates follow the standard Coptic/Julian calendar arithmetic (12 months of 30 days, plus a short 5- or 6-day 13th month).

        
remove
Coptic Easter uses the Alexandrian computus (the same reckoning as Eastern Orthodox Easter), converted from the Julian to the Gregorian calendar. Sham El Nessim is always the following Monday.

      

    


    

      
Egyptian holiday pack

      

        
remove
Fixed national days use their official Gregorian date. Coptic Christmas, Coptic Easter, and Sham El Nessim are computed from the rules above.

        
remove
Islamic holidays (Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Arafat Day, Islamic New Year, Mawlid) are marked as estimated, for the same moon-sighting reason as above.

        
remove
Dates shown are the canonical holiday itself — not any specific year's administrative "moved to Thursday" adjustment, which is a yearly Cabinet decision rather than a fixed rule.

      

    


    

      
Prayer times

      

        
remove
Method: Egyptian General Authority of Survey (Fajr 19.5°, Isha 17.5° below the horizon) — the standard convention used for Egypt.

        
remove
Asr uses the Shafi convention (shadow length equals object height), not the Hanafi convention (twice the height), which runs Asr later.

        
remove
Computed from real sun-position astronomy for each city's exact coordinates, then converted to that city's own local time zone.

        
remove
These are estimates, typically accurate to within a minute or two of your local mosque's posted times — always defer to the mosque for the exact call to prayer.
